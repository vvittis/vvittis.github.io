I am a PhD student at UMass Amherst, advised by Alexandra Meliou and Peter J. Haas. My research is on in-database prescriptive analytics, in particular package queries and their incremental maintenance under evolving data and constraints. More broadly, I am interested in database systems, query optimization, and machine learning for data management.

Before UMass, I completed an MEng/BSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Technical University of Crete, working with Antonios Deligiannakis on random forests under data drift.
